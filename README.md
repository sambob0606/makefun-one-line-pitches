## The entry

One sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a friend who has
never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun:

> Tell an AI agent the app idea you've been carrying in your head for months, and watch it build a working first version while you finish your coffee — no code, no setup, just your idea finally real.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes that sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`. The sealed scorer gates it: UTF-8 text, 1–240
characters, not only white space. A house judge then compares entries two
at a time on persuasiveness, clarity, truth, warmth and craft.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
